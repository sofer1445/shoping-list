# Shopping application redesign

## Scope
Operate: existing Hebrew mobile-first household shopping application. Prioritize in-store clarity and touch comfort; preserve all existing data flows and navigation.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Clear household wayfinding: product and purchase action first, not layers of decorative panels.
OWN-WORLD: Cloud-white canvas, ink text, crisp cobalt navigation, fresh green completion, pale blue selection, warm amber only for warnings. Preserve Sora/Manrope with a readable system Hebrew fallback. Small-radius list cards and distinct full-width data sections.
STORY: Choose a household list, scan grouped products, enter an immersive product view, finish or carry remaining items forward, then review history and insights.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact brand header, title above a prominent new-list action, separate owned/shared groups and readable progress. Focus view has only exit and progress above the product, a quiet category label, large unclipped product name, and a generous bottom purchase button. Signature move: a crisp green check and progress change on purchase, with reduced-motion fallback.
FORM: Grounded candidate 6, household/park wayfinding translated to standard product controls; seed: 9991a84ae1d078ae. Keep the established Cloud White and feed commitments. Challenger verdicts: boarding-pass competitive on clarity but less household resonance; data-sublime, annual plate, catalog sleeve, dance notation and streaming wall declined on both axes. Raises: data-sublime numerical alignment, boarding-pass stable row identity, annual plate spacing discipline, catalog sleeve attention restraint, dance notation balanced action area, streaming wall current-product prominence. No borrowed decorative motifs.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
